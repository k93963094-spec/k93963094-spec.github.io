About Us

An Omani company delivering civil, pipeline, oil & gas field, telecom, equipment, manpower and supply services.

Our company

Murtafat Aswad Global (MAT)

Murtafat Aswad Global — Murtafat Aswad Al-Alamiua LLC — is an Omani company serving oil & gas operators, telecom providers, contractors and developers, with locations in Ghala (Muscat) and Hamra Droo (Ibri).

We adopt a customer-centric approach, working closely with clients to understand their needs and deliver customised solutions that drive value and growth. Our integrated approach brings together logistics, construction and technical expertise to provide comprehensive solutions that streamline project execution and minimise risk.
